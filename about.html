What is Aguinaldo International School?

Aguinaldo International School (AIS) is a private, secular, and coeducational international school located in Ermita, Manila, Philippines. We offer education from nursery to senior high school while simultaniously providing a Special Education (SpEd) class for those who are neurodivergient or experience disabilities.




Our Curriculum

Our curriculum combines national standards with American Core Standdards with international frameworks such as McRel and the International Baccalaureate Programme.


Mission and Vision

Our school's mission is to raise each child to their fullest potential with individualized instruction in a diverse, nurturing, and motivating environment.

Our vision, on the other hand, is to be a values-oriented international school that develops globally competent students.


Our Core Values

Virtus, Excellentia, Servitium – Virtue, Excellence, Service


Our Student Profile Goals

  1. Knowledgeable learners who are adept at finding, verifying, organizing, recalling, and applying factual information.
  2. Understanding learners who use critical thinking to solve problems and make sound judgments/decisions/arguments.
  3. Globally aware learners who understand global issues, value diversity, and act to protect nature and human rights.
  4. Expressive learners who speak, write, and present with clarity, fluency, accuracy, depth, feeling, and personal voice.
  5. E-literate learners who are adept at creating, sharing, accessing, & organizing accurate information using ideal tools and technology.
  6. Motivated learners who set learning goals, manage time, self-reflect, adjust strategies, and overcome setbacks.
  7. Collaborative learners who are joyful, supportive, respectful, dependable, & ready to share (e.g. resources, ideas & views).
  8. Courageous learners who are open-minded, innovative, adaptable, inquisitive, and seek help, feedback & challenge.
